Which feed is wrong, and why

Two feeds report the same three meters for November 2026. Source B passes a month-level count (2,880 rows, exactly 30 × 96), yet it comes up 200.6 kWh short of the register reads across the three meters. Counting intervals per day, before any cleaning, finds the cause: a 6-hour collector outage on Nov 18. Along the way it keeps the repeated DST hour on Nov 1 that a naive cleanup would have deleted.

5Read the gap pattern

Every interval missing from cleaned Source B falls in one contiguous run per meter, on the same day and at the same time for all three meters. A run of 4 or more intervals points to a communication outage rather than a device fault, and a shared run across meters points to the collector. This answers why.
