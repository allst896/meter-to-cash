Meter-to-cash demo · Stage 4 of 5: Invoicing

Right numbers, wrong page

Every invoice ties to its statement to the cent. Two of them still fail the customer. On INV-202701-1001 the November true-up is printed as December energy, and on INV-202701-1003 it is a bare $12.97 “Adjustment” with nothing to say what it is for. Neither is a wrong number. Both come from how the invoicing system builds the page, so a tie-out can't see them, but a customer checking the bill would.

2Classification

Each printed line must sit in the section its statement line belongs in. One doesn't.

InvoicePrinted asAmountStatement line typeBelongs inResult
INV-202701-1001Current: “Energy”−$7.11TRUE_UPAdjustment✓ Wrong section

The commercial invoice template maps true-ups into current charges as “Energy.” INV-202701-1001 shows December current charges of $2,443.43 when December's actual charges were $2,450.54, so the customer is told they used less than they did, and the November correction disappears from view. Residential and industrial templates map true-ups correctly; one bad mapping mislabels every invoice that uses it. Fix the template.

4Adjustment references

A printed prior-period adjustment must say which invoice it adjusts, for what service period, and the billed and actual quantities behind it.

INV-202701-1003Adjustment+$12.97——✓ No original invoice; no service period; no billed vs actual quantities

The true-up looks up its original invoice in the register, but only among invoices with status ISSUED. Credit CR-0003 marked A-1003's December invoice ADJUSTED, so the lookup came back empty and the template fell back to a bare “Adjustment.” The amount is right and the invoice ties out; the customer just can't tell what $12.97 is for. Fix the lookup to include adjusted invoices, then reissue.
