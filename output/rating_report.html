M3 delivery moved +22.2% while its usage moved −2.3%. Same consumption, different price: a grouping or category change, not a usage change. The zone history reads Aug LZ_HOUSTON → Sep LZ_HOUSTON → Oct LZ_HOUSTON, then LZ_WEST in November, from an account update on Nov 1 with no move or service transfer behind it. Volume conservation passes because every kWh is priced, and the system comparison passes because the wrong zone was migrated into the new engine too. Fix: correct the account setup and rebill ($147.04 back to the customer for November).

The new engine priced November usage with a rate that starts after November ends: it looks up rates by run date. Fix the new engine to rate by service period before cutover. The same error reaches the January true-up, which is recalculated after the cutoff; here it is small because both November runs fall after December 1 and carry the same wrong rate, so only the on-peak kWh that changed between the initial and final data is mispriced.
