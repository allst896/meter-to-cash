The balance ties. The interest doesn't.

All three January 1 statements roll forward to the cent. Yet A-1001 is charged $1.28 too much interest, because a $1,300.00 payment that arrived Dec 18 was keyed as Dec 28. The amount was right, so nothing else looked wrong. Only recalculating the interest independently, then checking the payment against the bank, shows it.

What billing picks up from rating

The December 1 bill was November's initial rating as production billed it, with M3 still in the wrong zone. Two adjustments arrive on January 1: the zone fix as credit CR-0003 ($124.90, effective Dec 15), and the true-up, which replaces metering's estimate with actuals. The bank received ACH-20261218-0417 on Dec 18, and the reference itself carries that date. The ledger entry was keyed on Dec 18 with a payment date of Dec 28, 10 days after the day it was entered, which is impossible. The payment record is wrong, not the interest formula. Fix: correct the date, recalculate, and the amount due falls from $3,340.41 to $3,339.13.
